Festival Rules

Please read the general rules before registering.

  1. All participants must provide accurate registration details.
  2. Carry your college identity card during the festival.
  3. Follow the timings and instructions provided by coordinators.
  4. Participants must respect other students, staff, and guests.
  5. Bring your own laptop or equipment if the event requires it.
  6. Follow workshop-specific instructions and safety guidelines.
  7. Report to the event venue at least 15 minutes before the start.
  8. The organizing committee's published event decisions will apply.
